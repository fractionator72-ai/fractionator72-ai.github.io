EIA-820 Annual Refinery Report, capacity as of January 1, . Barrels per day unless a column says otherwise.

Notes

"U.S." is PAD I–V (50 states and D.C.), matching the published report. PAD VI (territories) appears only in 2022 (Limetree Bay, U.S. Virgin Islands) and is excluded unless the box is checked.

"Refineries" counts every operable site, including six process operators with no crude distillation unit (Enterprise and Targa Mont Belvieu, Equistar Channelview, Pasadena Performance Products, Excel Paralubes Westlake, Trecora Silsbee). "Idle" means crude distillation completely idle (idle capacity > 0 and no operating capacity); non-crude operators count as operating.

The workbooks carry no facility ID. Facilities are linked across years by state and site name, adding the operator when more than one company shares a site name. Renames and ownership changes can split a facility's history (for example Woods Cross, Anacortes, Toledo, Channelview, Billings). Operator and corporation names are normalized by spelling only, so a merger shows as a new name.

Next-year values are what respondents projected on the survey. Stream-day capacity of nonrespondents is derived as calendar day ÷ 0.95 and their downstream and production capacity is carried forward. Hydrogen is MMcf/day and sulfur is short tons/day. Zero values are not stored in the source and display as 0.
